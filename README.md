X-linked adrenoleukodystrophy (X-ALD) is a serious genetic disorder marked by the buildup of very long-chain fatty acids (VLCFAs) due to mutations in the ABCD1 gene. This study examines the clinical, metabolic, genetic, and pathophysiological aspects of X-ALD, with a focus on the detrimental effects of VLCFA accumulation on adrenal and nervous system function. Utilizing a multi-omics approach, we identified potential biomarkers that improve the accuracy of diagnosis and effectiveness of therapeutic interventions for X-ALD. Our findings underscore the crucial role of early diagnosis and personalized treatment plans in slowing disease progression and improving patient outcomes. This research offers vital insights into the molecular mechanisms of X-ALD and opens new pathways for future therapeutic advancements
